People sometimes ask what's actually running behind the radio. The short version: less than you'd think.

## The pieces

1. **The library.** Tracks are tagged and sorted into playlists by mood and tempo.
2. **The scheduler.** It picks what plays next so that a slow jam doesn't land right after a floor-filler.
3. **The encoder.** It turns the audio into a stream that browsers and the iOS app can play.
4. **This website.** It shows what's playing now and lets you hit play.

The web player polls for the current track and updates the cover art and progress bar as songs change. If you want to poke at it, the player lives in `src/components/radio/`.
