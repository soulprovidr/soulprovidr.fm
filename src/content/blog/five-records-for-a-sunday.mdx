Some records are for dancing. These are for the morning after.

## The list

1. **Bill Withers – _Just As I Am_.** Warm, plainspoken and perfectly paced.
2. **Marvin Gaye – _What's Going On_.** Still as urgent and as gorgeous as ever.
3. **Minnie Riperton – _Come to My Garden_.** Lush arrangements built for slow mornings.
4. **D'Angelo – _Voodoo_.** Loose, deep and best heard from start to finish.
5. **Erykah Badu – _Baduizm_.** Unhurried and confident, with a groove you can sink into.

> Put the first one on, make a second coffee, and don't look at your phone until side B.
