Saturday was grey and cold, which is the best weather for flipping through records. I started at the north end of the market and worked my way down.

## What came home with me

- A scratched-up soul 45 with a B-side that's better than the A-side
- A library funk record with no sleeve, bought on the strength of the drummer's name alone
- A disco twelve-inch the shop owner insisted I take

> The best finds are always in the bin nobody else bothered to look through.
